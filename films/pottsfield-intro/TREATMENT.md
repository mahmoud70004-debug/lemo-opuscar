# Pottsfield Studio — Official 10-Second Game Boot Intro (`pottsfield-intro`)

## 1. Three Candidate Structures & Choice

1. **Candidate A — *The Lantern in the Woods* (Environment-first approach)**
   A wide shot of a dark autumn forest narrows through misty branches until the camera discovers a glowing pumpkin sitting on a stump, which then dissolves into the circular Pottsfield Studio crest.
   *Why rejected:* Takes too long to reach the hero pumpkin in a 10-second boot intro, and uses a dissolve rather than keeping the exact centerpiece pumpkin anchored as the continuous hero.

2. **Candidate B — *Carved in 2D, Ignited in 3D, Crowned by the Crest* (Chosen)**
   Out of velvet darkness, white-amber woodcut strokes carve the exact Pottsfield Evil Pumpkin in crisp 2D relief (0–2.2s). Everything hushes into near-silence; a single mischievous spark glints in its left eye (2.75s) and **ignites on Beat 6 (3.0s)**, inflating the flat 2D ink into a 3D normal-mapped lantern pouring volumetric orange god-rays and floating depth-of-field embers into the night. As the camera cranes and pulls back (4.75–6.2s), the Hero Pumpkin settles directly into its exact throne at the bottom-center of the official Pottsfield Studio emblem while autumn vines, maple leaves, companion pumpkins, the crimson ring, and *Pottsfield STUDIO* bloom around it (6.0–7.4s), holding alive and radiant until a clean game-boot fade to black (9.45–10.0s).
   *Why chosen:* Immediately hooks in < 0.3s, visually embodies the studio's dual **2D + 3D** identity through a single unforgettable medium-native transformation, keeps the exact logo centerpiece pumpkin continuously on screen *"as-is"*, and locks seamlessly into the full official emblem.

3. **Candidate C — *Mischievous Blink Montage* (Rapid cuts)**
   Five rapid flashes of pumpkins blinking open in different corners of the screen before slamming all five into the final emblem.
   *Why rejected:* Scatters focus away from the single iconic centerpiece pumpkin and feels frantic rather than atmospheric and enchanting.

## 2. Logline, Arc & Brief Notes

- **Logline:** A flat 2D woodcut carving of the wicked Pottsfield pumpkin catches a single autumn spark, erupting into warm 3D volumetric firelight before summoning the full autumn wreath and *Pottsfield STUDIO* crest around itself.
- **Arc:**
  - **Setup (0.00s – 2.75s):** Tactile 2D relief lines carve the wicked pumpkin silhouette out of pitch darkness, ending in a tense, breathless silence.
  - **Turn / Native Move (2.75s – 6.00s):** A spark in the hollow eye ignites inner fire — transforming 2D hand-cut lines into rich 3D form, subsurface orange warmth, volumetric god-rays, and 3D parallax embers as the camera pulls back.
  - **Ending (6.00s – 10.00s):** Landing on its exact seat, the glowing centerpiece pumpkin awakens the swirling vines, companion pumpkins, autumn leaves, crimson circle, and *Pottsfield STUDIO* wordmark into the iconic official lockup, then fades cleanly to black for game boot handoff.
- **Voice & Subtitles:** **None** (per studio direction: pure iconic musical sting + tactile sound design for universal game-boot playback across languages and platforms).
- **Outputs:**
  - `pottsfield-intro.mp4` — **1920×1080** (16:9 Landscape for Windows & landscape games), 30 fps, −14 LUFS
  - `pottsfield-intro-vertical.mp4` — **1080×1920** (9:16 Portrait for mobile vertical games), 30 fps, −14 LUFS

## 3. Benchmarks (Learn / Don't Take)

1. ***Over the Garden Wall* Title Sequence (Cartoon Network, dir. Patrick McHale, 2014)** & **Supergiant Games / Team Cherry Boot Logos**
   - **What to learn:** Deep velvet blacks (`#000000`–`#080403`) where warm lantern orange (`#F4730A`) and dried-leaf crimson (`#B52B28`) carry all atmospheric emotion; nostalgic folk-mystery acoustic instrumentation (plucked strings + celesta/music-box + warm low bowed resonance); unhurried, confident final logo hold that primes the player for an atmospheric world.
   - **What not to take:** Never borrow *Over the Garden Wall* characters, specific melodies, typography, or illustrations; use strictly Pottsfield Studio's own Evil Pumpkin and official emblem artwork.

2. **Playdead / Lumrook Studio Ident Grammar**
   - **What to learn:** Single clear physical action (spark → ignition → architectural lockup) readable even on a small phone screen; crisp foley-to-light synchronization; clean black tail for seamless transition into any game title screen.
   - **What not to take:** No cold monochrome or purple sci-fi/minimalist palette, no crow/chess imagery.

## 5. Second-by-Second Beat Sheet (120 BPM · 30 fps)

- **`0.00 – 0.25s`:** Velvet black screen; cold autumn wind whispers in early (J-cut) as 3 faint dry-ash specks drift across the frame.
- **`0.25 – 1.00s`:** First woodcut gouge strikes at the twisted stem! Crisp pale-amber/ivory 2D relief strokes carve downward along the stem ridges and upper pumpkin ribs (stepped at 15 fps).
- **`1.00 – 2.15s`:** Hard print-plate cut to frontal Close-Up. Dual symmetrical gouges slice out the angry angled eyes, sharp nose triangle, and wicked 9-tooth grin.
- **`2.15 – 2.75s`:** **[REAL SILENCE #1 — The Breath]** Carving ceases. Near-total silence (-58 dBFS room air). The stark 2D carved pumpkin stares out of the black as the camera creeps closer.
- **`2.75 – 3.00s`:** *TING!* A haunted celesta grace note rings as a bright orange spark pops inside the left eye cavity, flicking a tiny arc of fire into the right eye (anticipation crouch/compress by 2%).
- **`3.00 – 4.75s`:** **[TURN / NATIVE MOVE — 2D → 3D IGNITION]** On Beat 6 (`3.00s`), deep hearth fire explodes inside the pumpkin! Warm Pottsfield Orange (`#F4730A`) floods across the shell with 3D normal-mapped shading, rim-light, and blazing volumetric god-rays pouring through the eyes, nose, and grin. Swirling 3D autumn embers with foreground bokeh rise around the face.
- **`4.75 – 5.65s`:** Camera cranes upward and pulls back smoothly while the Hero Pumpkin glides toward the lower-center seat of the crest, trailing warm glowing embers.
- **`5.65 – 6.00s`:** **[NEAR-SILENCE #2 — Pre-Lockup Inhale]** Musical phrase suspends in reverb air; reverse-ember shimmer pulls inward toward the pumpkin's core.
- **`6.00 – 6.85s`:** **Crest Bloom!** On Beat 12 (`6.00s`), the Hero Pumpkin locks into its exact crest position with a warm sub-bass & wood-hearth thump; crimson and orange vines spiral outward from its base, the 4 companion pumpkins ignite left and right, maple leaves unfurl, and the crimson outer ring sweeps shut overhead with tiny stars and moths.
- **`6.50 – 7.35s`:** The gothic arched **"Pottsfield"** wordmark carves & blazes across the upper arch in glowing orange ember light, followed at `7.25s` by **"S T U D I O"** locking in crimson below it.
- **`7.35 – 9.45s`:** **Official Lockup Hold (2.10s):** Full *Pottsfield STUDIO* emblem holds in crisp perfection while inner lantern fire gently flickers inside the pumpkins and warm embers drift upward.
- **`9.45 – 10.00s`:** Smooth fade to `#000000` black as the final harmonic overtone decays cleanly to zero.

## 8. Title & Brand Fidelity Rules

- **No redrawn stand-ins for the brand:** Both the standalone Evil Pumpkin (`assets/pumpkin_hero.png`) and the full official crest (`assets/logo_full.png`) come directly from Pottsfield Studio's supplied high-resolution artwork.
- **Exact Hero Continuity:** The standalone Evil Pumpkin is aligned seamlessly with its exact counterpart inside `logo_full.png` (`scale = 0.4350`, `center = (621.5, 912.0)` in the `1254×1254` emblem canvas) so the viewer sees one continuous hero character from first woodcut stroke to final studio lockup.
- **Safe Framing in Both Aspect Ratios:**
  - **16:9 (`1920×1080`):** Final crest diameter `~840 px` (`78%` of frame height), centered cleanly with generous breathing space.
  - **9:16 (`1080×1920`):** Final crest diameter `~860 px` (`80%` of frame width), centered optically slightly above geometric midpoint (`y = 48.5%`) with vertical volumetric light shafts and rising embers filling the tall mobile frame naturally.
