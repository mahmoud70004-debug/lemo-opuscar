### Step 2: Important Rule About YouTube's "Altered Content / AI" Checkbox
During upload on YouTube Studio, you will see *"Altered content: Do any of the following describe your content?"*
- **Select `No`** for stylized game art, animated logos, shaders, and vibe-coded gameplay!
- *Why:* Per YouTube's official policy, `"Yes"` is **only** required for photorealistic synthetic video/audio that makes a *real person* appear to say/do something they didn't, or fakes a *real-world news event/place*. Selecting `"Yes"` on stylized game trailers needlessly puts a misleading deepfake warning banner over your video.

### Step 3: How to Engineer 100k+ Views on YouTube Shorts (The 2 Golden Metrics)
YouTube's Shorts algorithm promotes a video from the initial **500–1,500 seed test** to **10,000–100,000+ global views** based on two numbers in YouTube Analytics:
1. **`Stayed to watch` (Viewed vs. Swiped Away) → Target: `> 72%`**
   - Viewers decide in the **first 1.2 seconds**. Even on your 10s intro, add a clean bold 1-line English hook in the upper black space during `0.0s – 2.5s` so viewers immediately have something intriguing to read while the woodcut stem carves!
2. **`Average Percentage Viewed (APV)` → Target: `> 115%` on a 10-second Short**
   - Because `pottsfield-intro-vertical.mp4` is **10.0 seconds** and fades smoothly from `#000000` black at `0.0s` to `#000000` black at `10.0s`, it **loops seamlessly**.
   - **The Pinned Comment Trick:** Immediately after publishing, post a **Pinned Comment** asking a specific gamer question (below). While players read and type replies, your 10-second Short loops 2–3 times in the background — shooting your APV to **140%–200%**!
