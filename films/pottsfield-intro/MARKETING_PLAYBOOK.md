## 4. Solving the "Egyptian IP / Arabic FYP → Western Audience" Problem

TikTok and Instagram use **completely different geo-routing systems**. Treating them the same is why 90% of MENA studios accidentally get locked into local traffic.

### A. TikTok (Strict Hardware/SIM Geo-Routing — Read Before Posting!)
TikTok determines a video's **initial 200–500 test viewers** using:
1. **SIM Card Country Code (MCC)** — *even if Mobile Data is off and you are on Wi-Fi, TikTok reads `MCC 602` (Egypt) from the SIM tray!*
2. **Device Region, System Language & Timezone**
3. **IP Address type** (Residential vs. flagged free VPN datacenter)

**How to reach US / UK / Global Gamers on TikTok from Egypt:**
1. **Before Posting Anything — Check Account Status:**
   - Since you just created the account and haven't posted yet: if it was created with an Egyptian SIM inside the phone, TikTok has likely tagged the account's home region as `EG`.
   - **Best Move (Takes 10 minutes now, saves 6 months later):** Dedicate one clean device (an old secondary Android/iPhone) as the **"Studio Publishing Phone"**:
     1. **Physically remove the Egyptian SIM card** (keep the SIM tray empty, OR insert an active **US/UK eSIM or physical US SIM** like Tello / Ultra Mobile PayGo / UK Giffgaff — costs ~$3–$5/mo and permanently gives you genuine US/UK SIM telemetry without needing risky VPNs!).
     2. If using empty SIM tray + Wi-Fi: use a **clean Static Residential US or UK IP** (never free VPNs — TikTok shadowbans shared datacenter VPN IPs at `0 views`, and never switch countries back and forth).
     3. Set Device **Language = English (US)**, **Region = United States**, and **Timezone = New York (EST)**.
     4. Create the fresh `@pottsfieldstudio` TikTok account inside this environment (you can rename the old empty account first so `@pottsfieldstudio` becomes free immediately).
2. **The 4-Day "FYP Training" Warm-Up (Before Post #1):**
   - Spend 10–15 minutes/day for 3–4 days searching **only** English indie game tags (`#indiedev`, `#indiegame`, `#mobilegaming`, `#hollowknight`, `#monumentvalley`, `#limbo`, `#gamedev`).
   - Watch English indie game devlogs to **100% completion**, save 5–10 of them, and leave genuine developer comments in English.
   - **Confirm success:** Once 90%+ of your *For You Page* is English indie games, narrative games, and Western gaming creators (zero Arabic/Egyptian local trends), your account graph is primed for Post #1!

### B. Instagram Reels (Interest & Content Graph — Much Friendlier from Egypt!)
Instagram does **NOT** lock your global reach by SIM card the way TikTok does. Instagram routes Reels by **Audio/Text Language + Visual Topic + Early Engager Graph**:
- Write **100% English** on-screen hooks, captions, hashtags, and image Alt-Text.
- Set Account Category to **`Video Game`** or **`Game Publisher`** (Creator or Business account).
- **CRITICAL GOLDEN RULE (Applies to BOTH TikTok & Instagram):**
  **NEVER share your new studio account with Egyptian/Arab friends, family, or local Facebook groups asking them to follow/like Post #1!**
  *Why:* If your first 40 followers and 30 likes are in Egypt, Instagram & TikTok's lookalike AI immediately classifies your studio as *"Local Egyptian Entertainment"* and sends your next 20 videos exclusively to Egypt! Let 100% of your first followers come organically from global `#indiedev` / `#mobilegaming` discovery (or a tiny $5 Lumrook US/UK seed boost).

### C. Add YouTube Shorts from Day 1 (Zero Geo-Lock!)
- Create a **Pottsfield Studio** YouTube channel (`Channel Country: United States`, `Video Language: English (US)`).
- YouTube Shorts completely ignores your physical Egyptian IP — 70–90% of views on English indie-game Shorts come from US/EU/Asia automatically, reusing the **exact same `1080×1920` MP4 files** with 2 minutes of extra upload work!
