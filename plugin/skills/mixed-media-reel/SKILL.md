# Mixed-Media Viral Reel & High-Impact VFX

When directing a vertical (`1080x1920`, `60fps`) viral Reel, TikTok, or YouTube Short, smooth easing curves alone are not enough—if 80% of the screen is empty solid colour with code-drawn rectangles, viewers scroll past in 1.5 seconds ("AI-slop wireframe syndrome").

## 1. Anti-"AI Wireframe" Rules
1. **Zero Dead Background**: Every frame needs layered depth:
   - **Far layer (`0.25x–0.35x` camera parallax)**: Full-bleed darkened, duotone-graded photo/screenshot mosaic or atmospheric environment plate + radial light bloom.
   - **Mid layer (`0.65x` parallax)**: Floating particles, volumetric light rays, halftone dots, or drifting secondary evidence cards.
   - **Hero layer (`1.0x`)**: Real screenshots, product captures, cutouts, and kinetic display type.
   - **Lens layer (`1.3x` + screen space)**: Close bokeh embers/sparks, film grain, anamorphic impact flares, chromatic aberration, and vignette.
2. **Real Proof Over Drawn Boxes**: Show the actual interface, actual ad, actual spreadsheet, or actual gameplay capture—then annotate, slice, magnify, or shatter it.

## 2. Signature Mixed-Media VFX Moves
- **Evidence Fan & Slam**: Deal 3–5 real screenshots onto the canvas at `-7°..+8°` tilt angles `80ms` apart with `easeOutBack` overshoot, heavy drop shadows, and a decaying camera shake (`A * exp(-14 * dt) * sin(48 * dt)`).
- **Freeze, Circle & Magnify (`Loupe`)**: Draw a glowing ring clockwise (`0.35s`) over the decisive detail in a screenshot, then pop open a circular `2.5x–4x` glass loupe cropped from the exact source pixels with crosshairs and specular rim.
- **Image-Mapped Polygon Shatter**: Pre-triangulate the composite frame into 20–30 convex shards around the impact point; on impact (`2-frame` bright flash + dead-silence drop), clip and affine/3D-transform each shard's real image texture outward with angular velocity and gravity, revealing the new world behind it.
- **Blade / Diagonal Cleave**: Clip a target card stack along a diagonal half-plane `(x - x0)*nx + (y - y0)*ny < 0` vs `>= 0`; sweep a molten light trail across the cut line and slide the two halves apart while emitting sparks along the seam.
- **Chromatic Aberration & Speed-Ramp Streaks**: During whip-pans and bass slams, offset red/blue or amber/crimson additive ghosts proportional to camera velocity (`vx, vy`) + impact trauma.

## 3. High-Octane Trailer Sound Design (`-14 LUFS`)
- **Rhythmic Engine**: A hype Reel requires **real rhythmic percussion**—not ambient pads alone:
  - Satire / fast critique: 130–140 BPM punchy sub-kick (`50–55 Hz` pitch-drop), crisp snare/clap on 2 & 4, 16th-note hi-hat momentum, gritty bass riff.
  - Pivot / drop: Tape-stop pitch plunge → `0.20–0.30s` **complete silence** → shatter + sub-bass boom (`40–45 Hz`).
  - Heroic / reveal act: Booming cinematic taiko/war-drum ensemble + 8th/16th-note spiccato string ostinato + brass/horn swells synced to visual slams.
- **Sidechain Ducking**: Duck music `-7 to -9 dB` under voiceover (`30ms` attack, `220ms` release) so narration cuts cleanly through heavy drums; let hits bloom in breath gaps.
